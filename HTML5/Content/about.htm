About

All content included on this webpage was created by UMass Amherst students enrolled in English 381 during the spring 2026 semester. A few students chose to use AI for purposes of revision and maintaining language consistency in the Transitions Effects and Audio Description topic pages. All other modules did not utilize AI in any way, shape or form.

Research was conducted with online materials listed below.
