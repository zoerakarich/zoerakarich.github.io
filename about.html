About Zoe Rakarich

Education

Zoe Rakarich graduated from the University of Massachusetts Amherst in May 2026. Before transferring into UMass Amherst, she started college at the University of Pittsburgh.

Experience

Throughout college, Rakarich interned for various media outlets, gaining hand-on experience in major cities.

As a Boston native, she spent a summer at WCVB Channel 5 on the assignment desk and the next at WBZ Channel 4 producing news scripts. During her junior year, Rakarich studied abroad in Barcelona, Spain where she edited articles, wrote a spotlight feature and managed the social media account of an arts and culture magazine. In her senior year, she did a semester in the nation's capital creating online content for FOX 5 DC.
